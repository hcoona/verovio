## Summary

Fixes #4611 (stacked on #3993 / `feat/pedal-line-bounce`).

1. Register and reset `AttLineRend` and `AttLineRendBase` on `vrv::Pedal`, wire `ReadLineRend` / `WriteLineRend` in `MEIInput` and `MEIOutput`, and add `Pedal::GetLineWidth` matching `BracketSpan` and `Octave`.
2. Render `@lform` (`dashed`, `dotted`, `wavy`, `solid`) and `@lwidth` in `View::DrawPedalLine` while keeping end hooks and bounce notches solid.
